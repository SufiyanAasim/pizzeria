# User Guide

PIZZEria is a browsing/ordering-intent site — there is nothing to sign
up for.

- **Home (`/`)** — brand intro and a preview of the pizza menu.
- **Full Menu (`/menu`)** — every category (Pizza, Pasta, Ramen,
  Sides, Dessert, Drinks). Tap/click a category tab to switch. "Add to
  Order" adds an item to your cart; pizza items open a topping picker
  first.
- **Cart** — the bag icon in the header shows your item count and
  opens a slide-in drawer to adjust quantities or remove items.
- **Checkout (`/checkout`)** — review your order and total, add your
  name and contact info, and send it — this opens your device's mail
  app with everything pre-filled. No payment is collected on the site.
- **About (`/about`)** — the kitchen's story.
- **Order (`/contact`)** — fill in your name, party size, and what
  you're after; submitting opens your device's mail app with the
  message pre-filled, ready to send to the restaurant.
- **Gallery (`/gallery`)** — every dish across all six categories,
  illustrated (real photography still pending).
- **Reserve (`/reserve`)** — request a table by date, time, and party
  size; opens your mail app the same way checkout does.
- **Locations (`/locations`)** — pick your nearest location; the
  footer (and this page) then show its address and hours.
- **Credits (`/credits`)** — who built the site and what it's built
  with.

The site works the same on mobile and desktop; the navigation
collapses into a menu button below the `md` breakpoint (~768px).
